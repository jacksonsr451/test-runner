Fixed :meth:`MonkeyPatch.delattr <testrunner.MonkeyPatch.delattr>`, :meth:`MonkeyPatch.setitem <testrunner.MonkeyPatch.setitem>` and :meth:`MonkeyPatch.delitem <testrunner.MonkeyPatch.delitem>` recording a stale undo entry when the underlying mutation failed, which could cause spurious errors during teardown. The undo entry is now recorded only after the mutation succeeds, matching the behavior of :meth:`MonkeyPatch.setattr <testrunner.MonkeyPatch.setattr>`.
