:meth:`Node.get_closest_marker <_testrunner.nodes.Node.get_closest_marker>` now also accepts a :class:`~testrunner.MarkDecorator` as its ``default``, for example ``item.get_closest_marker("foo", testrunner.mark.foo(1))``, and returns the wrapped :class:`~testrunner.Mark`.

Previously the only way to build a ``default`` was to instantiate :class:`~testrunner.Mark` directly, which is private and warns.
