Passing a non-string value to a ``string``-typed ini option now emits a ``testrunner.TestrunnerRemovedIn10Warning``. This affects ``[tool.testrunner.ini_options]`` in ``pyproject.toml``, where TOML arrays are kept as lists by ``make_scalar`` instead of being converted to strings. In testrunner 10 this will raise a ``TypeError``, matching the behavior of the corresponding TOML config path.
