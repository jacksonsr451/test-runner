Contact channels
================

Web
---

- `testrunner issue tracker`_ to report bugs or suggest features.
- `testrunner discussions`_ at GitHub for general questions.
- `testrunner on stackoverflow.com <http://stackoverflow.com/search?q=testrunner>`_
  to post precise questions with the tag ``testrunner``.  New questions will usually
  be seen by testrunner users or developers and answered quickly.

Chat
----

- `testrunner discord server <https://discord.com/invite/testrunner-dev>`_
  for testrunner development visibility and general assistance.
- ``#testrunner`` `on irc.libera.chat <ircs://irc.libera.chat:6697/#testrunner>`_ IRC
  channel for random questions (using an IRC client, or `via webchat
  <https://web.libera.chat/#testrunner>`_)
- ``#testrunner`` `on Matrix <https://matrix.to/#/#testrunner:matrix.org>`_.

Microblogging
-------------

- Bluesky: `@testrunner.org <https://bsky.app/profile/testrunner.org>`_
- Mastodon: `@testrunner@fosstodon.org <https://fosstodon.org/@testrunner>`_
- Twitter/X: `@testrunnerdotorg <https://x.com/testrunnerdotorg>`_
